About Me

Building AI that actually gets used.

I got into AI through UX research. Quantitative studies, design systems, behavior analysis. At some point I noticed both need the same core skill. You have to understand how people actually work before you can build something useful for them.

At Müller Merkle Immobilien I noticed that a lot of research workflows were repetitive and saw the automation potential. I proposed AI automations on my own initiative, built the agents in LangChain with a Streamlit frontend, deployed them locally, and the tool ended up actually being used for market research. I also took the proposal to management myself. That's the part I'm most proud of.

What I bring is a mix of technical depth and business sense. Quantitative research at IONOS and 1&1, hands-on AI development with LangChain, n8n and RAG, and a double Master's in Data Engineering and Computer Science.
